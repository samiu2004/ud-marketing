    Pricing questions, answered.

    Are state filing fees included in the package prices?+

    No. Basic, Standard, and Premium have separate UD Marketing service fees. The applicable government filing fee depends on the state selected.

    How can I calculate my total LLC formation cost?+

    Select your state and package using the LLC Filing Fee Checker. It will show the package fee, listed state filing fee, and estimated combined total.

    Is the registered agent included?+

    Yes. All three fixed-price packages include registered-agent service for the first year. Subsequent renewals are separate and subject to the provider's applicable charges.

    What is the difference between a US Virtual Address and a Unique Virtual Address?+

    Basic includes a US virtual address. Standard and Premium include a unique virtual address with an assigned suite number, subject to provider availability and terms.

    What happens after the three-month Business Presence Package ends?+

    Customers can continue the US phone number, domain, or business email services by paying the applicable provider charges. Otherwise, the complimentary services terminate according to the provider's terms.

    What is the difference between a personal USD account and a business USD account?+

    Standard includes assistance with a personal USD account under the customer's name through an eligible provider such as Nsave or Elevate Pay. Premium includes assistance with a Payoneer business account under the company's name. Account approval and availability depend on the financial service provider.

    Is a USD account guaranteed?+

    No. UD Marketing provides application and setup assistance. The relevant financial institution or provider makes the final eligibility and approval decision.

    What is a Certificate of Good Standing?+

    It is a document issued by the relevant state authority confirming the entity’s standing under applicable state rules. Availability, timing, and terminology vary by state.

    Can I purchase individual services instead of a package?+

    Yes. Use the Custom option or visit the Services page to discuss individual requirements, including ITIN assistance and Amazon or e-commerce consultation.

    Are there additional annual state obligations?+

    Depending on the state, an LLC may have annual or biennial reports, registration fees, franchise taxes, or other obligations. These are separate from the initial formation cost displayed in the calculator.
