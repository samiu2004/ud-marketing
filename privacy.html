What this website collects

This version of the UD Marketing website has no account registration, upload form, payment form, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or application database. Guide searches, filters, and topic choices run in your browser and are not saved by the site.

Hosting and technical data

Your hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. When hosted on Cloudflare, consult Cloudflare’s privacy policy ↗ (opens in a new tab) for its data practices.

WhatsApp, email, and external links

Contact buttons take you to WhatsApp or your email application. Your message is not sent automatically. Those services process information under their own policies. Government resource links open third-party websites with their own privacy practices.

Information you choose to share

UD Marketing receives information you send directly to our business contact channels and uses it to discuss your inquiry and any service you request. Start with general business details. Do not send passwords, security codes, full tax identifiers, or identity documents in an initial inquiry. Before sharing sensitive records, ask us to explain the required purpose and appropriate delivery method.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion requests concerning information you shared directly, email UDLLC15@gmail.com. Any service-specific handling or retention requirements should be discussed before providing documents.
