GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation free?+

Yes. Submitting an inquiry and discussing your requirements does not require payment.

Can I start a US LLC if I don’t live in the United States?+

Non-US residents can generally form US LLCs, subject to applicable state requirements and individual circumstances.

Do I need to choose a package before contacting you?+

No. You can select “Not Sure Yet” and discuss your requirements during consultation.

Can I request only one service?+

Yes. UD Marketing offers individual services alongside bundled formation packages.

Can you guarantee EIN issuance or bank account approval?+

No. Government agencies and financial institutions control their own decisions and processing requirements.

What happens after the consultation?+

We explain the proposed scope, applicable charges, and next steps before you decide whether to proceed.

Will I need to upload documents immediately?+

No. The initial inquiry form only requests basic information. Additional documents may be requested later when appropriate.
